# E118: Did ideology move candidates off their polls?

Objective: a worked example (E118, midterms series) and a companion blog post that test whether Democratic candidates over- or under-performed their pre-election polls by ideology, once opponent strength, party support and the way ideology is assigned are accounted for.
Branch: `e118-ideology-poll-error` (worktree `coding_challenge-e118`, based on `origin/main` 1e695b9). Blog post in the `redam94.github.io` repo on its own branch.
Delivery: one commit per repository unit, following this repo's "Add E###" precedent. Push, PR and merge are the owner's decisions. Runner: `tools/memguard.py --max-gb 3 -- .venv/bin/python tools/build.py E118`.

## Specs

- **S1. The claim under test.** "many pundents say that centerist democrats over perfomed thier polling while further left democrates or DSA candidates under performed and it was because of their ideology". The analysis must be able to come out for the pundits or against them. Neither answer is assumed.
- **S2. Opponent strength.** Account for "how likeable/electable thier opponent was". Assumption: the available measures are proxies (opponent incumbency, opponent money, and any dated, sourced measure the data task finds). The text must say they are proxies.
- **S3. Party support.** Account for "how much support they recieved from the national party in financial and electoral support". Assumption: measured as candidate receipts and independent expenditures, with national party committees and aligned super PACs separated where the FEC data allow.
- **S4. Post hoc buckets.** Address that "the selection of ideological bucket a candidate is put in seems to be post hoc after observing the difference in margin between pre election polling and what the actual ballot margin was". Ideology must come from sources dated before each election, and the example must show how much an after-the-fact assignment can manufacture.
- **S5. Elections.** Owner's choice: "Add partial 2026 now". Main estimate on 2018 and 2022 (polls and certified results). A provisional 2026 section uses "whatever uncertified counts I can collect for polled races today", labelled as incomplete and uncertified, with a scorer that reruns when certified results are added.
- **S6. Deliverables.** "a blog post and an associated pymc-challenges notebook": E118 built and registered under this repo's AUTHORING.md contract, and a blog post with figures in the blog repo that links to it.

Acceptance: the E118 build succeeds under the memory guard and the runtime budget; every sentence of commentary agrees with the printed outputs and figures; every data file is registered with source and licence; every ideology flag traces to a dated source row; the blog post's numbers are the notebook's printed outputs.
