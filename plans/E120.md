Final test half: 15.5% lost against 13.8%, mean wait 63 s against 60, 58.9% waiting against 58.5%. Left open and said so in the notebook: the simulator stays too gloomy early and late in the day, and the conversion from agents answering to heads (0.64 over the day) is the least certain number.
