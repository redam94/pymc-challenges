## Specs

- **S1. Several models working together.** "unexpected topics or ways to show multiple models working together to solve a problem".
- **S2. Different from the existing examples.** "they should be different to what is already present in the examples".
- **S3. New ways to plot and present.** "I want to expand the way results are plotted and presented".
- **S4. Every step explained, with its alternatives.** "it should include multiple modeling steps that would be present when answering complex problems with explainations as to why each modeling step was taken and the alternatives that were consider to provide a guide for modelers".
- **S5. Not a straight line.** "show that often analysis are not a straight line and do involve choices". The three devices of E120, as proposed and accepted: a route map up front ("a figure of the analysis path, with dead ends drawn in"), a decision box per step ("what we chose, what we rejected, and what would change our mind"), a closing multiverse plot ("how the answer moves under each rejected alternative"). A proposed detour is a hypothesis: the notebook reports what the data show, and says so when the proposal was wrong (E120's plan, L5).
- **S6. E121, "Did they cheat? (online chess)".** Chain: "rating that drifts over time -> move-quality model given rating and position difficulty -> per-game surprise score -> base-rate correction -> accusation threshold with a false-accusation cost". Detour: "a simple z-score flags every fast-improving junior, and even a good detector mostly accuses innocent players when cheating is rare". New plots: "per-move 'accuracy fingerprints', accusation cost curves with posterior bands". Assumptions: data from Lichess open data (CC0), which is very large, so a streamed or sampled subset within the 8 GB machine; the source and licence are unverified. No real account is named or accused: account names are replaced by codes in the derived data, and the text speaks of patterns, not people.
- **S7. E122, "Why do three buses arrive together? (bus bunching)".** Chain: "segment travel times -> dwell time as a function of the gap to the bus ahead -> coupled headway dynamics -> counterfactual holding policy". Detour: "modelling segments independently fits well yet can never produce bunching; the feedback loop has to be modelled". New plots: "time-space (Marey) diagrams with posterior ribbons, headway phase portraits". Assumption: MBTA arrival and departure open data for one route; source and licence unverified.
- **S8. E123, "What did the wolf eat? (isotope mixing)".** Chain: "instrument calibration -> hierarchical source signatures -> Dirichlet mixing model -> value of adding another tracer". Detour: "when sources line up in isotope space the posterior is a ridge, so you choose between an informative prior, merging sources, or a third tracer". New plots: "isotope-space maps with source ellipses, ternary posterior densities". Assumption: the MixSIAR example datasets or a published isotope dataset; source and licence unverified. If no real calibration data exist, the calibration step is replaced by the step the data do support, and the text says so.
- **S9. E124, "Same person twice? (record linkage)".** Chain: "match/non-match mixture on field agreements -> one-to-one matching constraint -> sampled linkages carried into a downstream regression". Detour: "hard-thresholded links look clean but bias the downstream estimate toward zero". New plots: "bipartite link graphs with edge opacity as match probability, the final estimate plotted against the linkage threshold". Assumption: a real dataset with known duplicates is preferred (the repository's rule is real data); RLdata10000 is synthetic and is a fallback that must be disclosed. Source and licence unverified.
- **S10. Order and publication.** "merge to main and push then add the next notebooks". Assumption: the push covers E120, already done; the four new notebooks are built and committed on `multi-model-series` and are published when the owner says so.

Acceptance, each notebook: the build succeeds under the memory guard (3 GB) and within the runtime budget of AUTHORING.md; every sentence of commentary agrees with the printed outputs and figures; every data file is registered with source and licence, and no licence is asserted that was not read; the three devices of S5 are present; claims about the literature are checked against a source or flagged as unverified in the writer's report.
